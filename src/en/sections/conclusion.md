# Conclusion

## Summary

Before reducing your environmental impact from generative AI, you need to understand the factors that influence it.
They can be grouped into 3 categories: the model, its hosting, and the tokens.

Tools exist to measure the impact of open models with precision and certainty.
The most powerful proprietary models, such as those from OpenAI, Google, or Anthropic, are black boxes and do not allow a certain measurement to be established.
Independent organizations are developing estimation methods based on many unverifiable assumptions, but which can be relied on to obtain orders of magnitude.

The calculator I developed alongside this article lets the end user of a chatbot quantify how much their impact is reduced by adopting good practices: [Environmental Impact Calculator for my conversation with the AI](https://felixmortas.com/ai-inf-calculator)

At their level, the three main levers they can pull instantly are:

- prioritizing a traditional web search, by disabling automatic AI searches.
- choosing a small model with low effort, suited to the task at hand
- controlling the amount of tokens processed as input and generated as output.

As a developer, the range of action is broader and touches on concepts that are invisible to the end user.

At every level, it is possible to drastically reduce the environmental impact of your use of AI, while also improving the quality of the responses produced.
